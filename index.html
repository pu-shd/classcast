How it works

  • Students: click Share next to your name, then choose the screen or window to share.
  • Presenter: click View next to a student to show their screen on the projector.
  • Sound is off; talk in the room. Only screens are shared, never webcams.

Teachers: create a class link with your roster, title or room password.
